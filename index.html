01

Fall detection, LSTM-SVM

Jul 2026

Skeleton-based fall detection from MediaPipe Pose. A hybrid LSTM-SVM compared against LSTM-only and SVM-only baselines, with a real-time demo on video and live webcam. Research internship at USM, now my undergraduate thesis.

PyTorch, scikit-learn, MediaPipe

github.com/dnjwna/fall-detection-lstm-svm
Read the findings
  • 149-dim scale and translation-invariant pose features; stacked LSTM 64→32; RBF SVM with calibrated probabilities.
  • Videos are split before windowing, so no clip leaks between train and test.
  • Within-dataset 5-fold CV: fall F1 of 0.69 to 0.80, and all three variants are statistically equivalent.
  • Zero-shot URFD to Le2i over 6 seeds: fall recall of about 0.58 to 0.66. That generalization gap is the main finding.
