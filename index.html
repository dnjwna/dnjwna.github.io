01

Fall detection, LSTM-SVM

Jul 2026

Skeleton-based fall detection from MediaPipe Pose. A hybrid LSTM-SVM compared against LSTM-only and SVM-only baselines, with a real-time demo on video and live webcam. Research internship at USM, now my undergraduate thesis.

PyTorch, scikit-learn, MediaPipe

* Findings *

Fall F1, within-dataset 5-fold CV
0.69–0.80
Hybrid vs LSTM-only vs SVM-only
equivalent
Fall recall, zero-shot URFD → Le2i, 6 seeds
≈0.58–0.66

That generalization gap is the main finding. 149-dim scale and translation-invariant pose features, stacked LSTM 64→32, RBF SVM with calibrated probabilities. Videos are split before windowing, so no clip leaks between train and test.
